1. Drag "@APP_FILE@" onto the "Applications" folder.

   macOS blocks it on the first double-click.
   → In Applications, RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) "@APP_FILE@", choose
     "Open", then confirm "Open".
   → macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later: double-click once, then open System
     Settings › Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" at the bottom.

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/@APP_FILE@"
